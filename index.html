INNOVATION & INTEGRITY

The Science of Photorealistic AI Try-On

How VOGUE AI achieves high-fashion cloth transfer while fiercely preserving human identity.

🛡️

Face & Identity Preservation

Unlike generative systems that replace the entire subject with a generic avatar, our architecture isolates the face, hair texture, natural skin tones, and facial expressions using high-dimensional facial landmark masks.

📐

Neural Garment Warping (IDM-VTON)

Garments are dynamically deformed and aligned to match the user's natural body posture, shoulder angle, and movement lines. Realistic fabric folding, creasing, and lighting shadows are synthesized naturally.

🔒

Privacy & Data Protection

Your uploaded photographs are strictly processed for your personal virtual try-on session. They are never shared publicly or added to public training pools. You have full control to delete your photos at any time.
